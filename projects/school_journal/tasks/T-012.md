# T-012 — Roles and permissions matrix

- **Owner:** Claude
- **Status:** PLANNED
- **Depends on:** T-010 (can start in draft form earlier)

## Goal
Define who can see and change what. Journal part first; attendance and meals roles are added after T-011.

## Roles to cover
Subject teacher, homeroom teacher, substitute teacher, administration, kitchen staff (later), owner/administrator of the system.

## Deliverable
A matrix in `docs/` of the app repository (or in the Hub until T-004 is done): for each role and each data area, whether it can read, create, edit or delete. This becomes the basis of Grok's RLS policies (T-023).

## Done when
The owner has approved the matrix.
